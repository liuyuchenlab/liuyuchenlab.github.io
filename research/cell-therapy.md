## 中文

面向免疫细胞治疗与干细胞治疗的临床转化研究。结合基因编辑与单细胞技术，优化细胞治疗产品的制备工艺，提升治疗的安全性与有效性，推动从实验室到临床的转化。

## English

Translational research on immune cell therapy and stem cell therapy. Combining gene editing and single-cell technology to optimize the manufacturing process of cell therapy products, improving therapeutic safety and efficacy, and advancing bench-to-bedside translation.
